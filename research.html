Measurement of proton-induced absolute production cross-section of 6.13, 6.92 and 7.12 MeV γ-rays from ¹⁶O(p,p′γ)¹⁶O reaction

J. Phys. G: Nucl. Part. Phys. 2024
ISSN 0954-3899 / 1361-6471 · IF 3.5 · DOI 10.1088/1361-6471/ad1fd3

Absolute production cross-sections of 6.13, 6.92 and 7.12 MeV γ-rays from the ¹⁶O(p,p′γ)¹⁶O reaction are reported for incident proton energies of 8–16 MeV. Angular distributions were measured at 9 MeV. A phenomenological optical model potential (OMP) was developed and optimised using elastic, polarisation and total reaction data. Nuclear structure effects — low-lying state couplings, resonances, and deformation — were included. Calculated cross-sections agree well with measurements, though finer details highlight complex roles of channel coupling and resonances at low energies.

Response of LaBr₂.₈₅Cl₀.₁₅:Ce, LaBr₃:Ce and NaI:Tl crystals to fast and thermal neutrons

Nucl. Instrum. Methods Phys. Res. A 2024
ISSN 0168-9002 · IF 1.5 · DOI 10.1016/j.nima.2024.169178

First report of the neutron response of the newly marketed LaBr₂.₈₅Cl₀.₁₅:Ce crystal. Direct response for fast and thermal neutrons was measured using a calibrated Am-Be source alongside LaBr₃:Ce and NaI:Tl references. GEANT4 simulations were used to isolate neutron events from γ-ray backgrounds. Fast neutron detection efficiency of all three detectors is reported.

Nucl. Instrum. Methods Phys. Res. A 2020
ISSN 0168-9002 · IF 1.5 · DOI 10.1016/j.nima.2020.164567

Thorough characterisation of a 1.5″×1.5″ Sr²⁺ co-doped LaBr₃:Ce crystal covering energy resolution, timing resolution, internal activity, and efficiency from 661.7 keV to 4.43 MeV. Precise determination of quadrupole and hexadecapole deformation parameters of the sd-shell nucleus ²⁸Si

Phys. Lett. B 2023
ISSN 0370-2693 · IF 4.3 · DOI 10.1016/j.physletb.2023.138120

Quasi-elastic scattering of ²⁸Si on ⁹⁰Zr near the Coulomb barrier, analysed with Bayesian coupled-channels calculations. Unambiguously confirms ²⁸Si as oblate (β₂ = −0.38 ± 0.01), in excellent agreement with electromagnetic probes. First determination of hexadecapole deformation (β₄ = +0.03 ± 0.01) via QEL scattering. Results agree with Skyrme-Hartree-Fock theory.

Quasi-elastic scattering for the nuclear ground state structure: an intriguing case of ³⁰Si

Phys. Lett. B 2026
ISSN 0370-2693 · IF 4.3 · DOI 10.1016/j.physletb.2026.140239

QEL scattering of ²⁸,³⁰Si off ⁹⁰Zr near the Coulomb barrier. While ²⁸Si is uniquely oblate, coupled-channels analysis of ³⁰Si shows oblate, prolate, and spherical descriptions are equally compatible with data — a potential signature of shape fluctuations. Shell-model calculations reveal a sudden structural change from ²⁸Si to ³²Si, consistent with ³⁰Si lacking a well-defined intrinsic shape.
